
To quantify the carbon mitigation potential of wood use and unravel its determinants, a systematic literature search using a Web Of Science query was used to compile a database that contained 1002 carbon balance estimates  from 118 publications. The Web Of Science query included keywords referring to wood products, carbon balance and a set of drivers, or forest sector practices among forest management, supply chain or technology. 
 
Papers were checked to fit seven inclusion criteria. The paper had to be published in a scientific journal in English. Next, it was the originality of the data was check excluding reviews and duplicated presentations of a dataset. The study design was checked to select studies presenting a change in one of the pre-identified practice drivers and a change in wood mobilisation or use, as well as ensuring that the same processes were accounted for for the reference and alternative scenario within a study. Finally selected studies had to contain quantitative estimates of carbon sequestration or emission, presenting a carbon variable that can be cumulated over a period of time. The inclusion check was independently done twice to ensure no paper was erroneously excluded. Review papers, although excluded from the database, were checked for cited publications that could be added to the database.
